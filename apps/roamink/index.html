GOOD TO KNOW

Before your boarding call.

When should I install my eSIM?+

You can install it before departure while you have reliable Wi-Fi. Activation timing depends on the plan, so follow the instructions shown with your eSIM.

Can I keep WhatsApp on my normal number?+

Yes. An eSIM can provide mobile data while your existing number remains on your primary SIM.

Do I need to remove my physical SIM?+

No. On compatible dual-SIM devices, the travel eSIM can be added alongside your physical SIM.

What if payment succeeds but delivery is delayed?+

Payment and provisioning are tracked separately, so an interrupted fulfillment never means you should pay twice.
