Frequently Asked Questions

What does ROAMINK sell?

ROAMINK sells digital travel eSIM data plans for supported destinations.

How do I order?

Choose a destination and plan, enter your contact details, continue to secure payment, and wait for payment confirmation before digital delivery.

How is the eSIM delivered?

After verified payment, installation information is delivered digitally using the contact details associated with the order.

Do I need an eSIM-compatible phone?

Yes. Your device must support eSIM and be carrier-unlocked. Check compatibility before purchase.

When should I install it?

You can usually install while connected to reliable Wi-Fi before departure. Follow the activation instructions supplied with your plan.

Can I keep WhatsApp on my normal number?

Yes. On compatible dual-SIM devices, your travel eSIM can provide data while your existing number remains on your primary SIM.

What if payment succeeds but delivery is delayed?

Contact support with your order email and payment reference. Do not pay again for the same order while the first payment is being reviewed.

How is my data protected?

ROAMINK only uses customer information needed for checkout, fulfillment, support, security, and legal obligations. Payment card details are handled by the payment provider rather than stored by ROAMINK.

Who operates ROAMINK?

ROAMINK is operated by PT NADMO STUDIO INDONESIA. Business and support details are available on the Contact page.
