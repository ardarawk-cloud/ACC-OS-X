Travel connected. Know the terms.

This is ROAMINK's launch policy framework. Commercial sales remain disabled until final contracting details, supplier rules, tax information and live support contacts are confirmed.

Terms of service

ROAMINK sells digital travel connectivity products delivered as eSIM profiles or secure installation access. Customers are responsible for confirming that their device supports eSIM, is carrier-unlocked and is compatible with the destination network before purchase.

Orders are considered paid only after the payment provider confirms a successful transaction to the backend. Digital fulfillment begins after verified payment.

Refund policy

Refund eligibility depends on the digital product state and the underlying supplier policy. Unprovisioned orders can be treated differently from eSIMs that have already been issued, installed, activated or consumed.

If payment succeeds but automated provisioning fails, the same paid order is recovered or reviewed. Customers must not be asked to pay a second time merely because fulfillment was interrupted.

Privacy

ROAMINK processes the minimum customer information needed to fulfill orders, provide secure order access, send transactional messages, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Payment card details are not stored by ROAMINK when payment is handled by the payment provider.

Service availability

Third-party payment systems, connectivity suppliers and local mobile networks may experience outages. Order state and delivery evidence are used to determine the appropriate recovery or refund path.
