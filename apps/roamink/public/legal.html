Terms & Conditions

ROAMINK provides digital travel eSIM products. Customers should confirm device compatibility, carrier-unlocked status, destination coverage, data allowance, and validity before purchase.

Orders are considered paid only after payment confirmation from the payment provider. Digital fulfillment begins after confirmed payment. Activation timing and network performance may vary by plan and local operator.

Customers should follow the installation instructions supplied with the eSIM and should contact support before deleting an issued eSIM when troubleshooting.

Refund Policy

Refund requests are reviewed according to the status of the digital order. Eligible cases can include duplicate payment, confirmed payment where the eSIM was not issued, or a verified technical failure that prevents delivery or use and cannot be resolved by support.

An eSIM that has already been issued, installed, activated, or consumed may not be refundable unless a verified service failure or applicable consumer rule requires otherwise.

To request review, contact ROAMINK support with the order email, payment reference, and a short description of the issue. Do not make a second payment for the same order while the case is being reviewed.
